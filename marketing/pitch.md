## 2. STANDARD PITCH — 2 minutes
*(Investor meeting opener, panel introduction, buyer demo)*

**The problem:**

Congo's specialty coffee has been one of the best-kept secrets in the industry.
Cooperatives in the Kivu region grow Bourbon and Arabica at 1,400 to 1,800 metres,
achieving cupping scores above 85 points — comparable to the finest Ethiopian and
Rwandan origins.

Yet their presence in the global specialty market is almost invisible.

Not because the quality isn't there. Because the infrastructure for visibility
wasn't there. Buyers received PDF files. Static documents. No search, no filter,
no comparison, no history, and nothing to share with a sourcing partner across
the world.

**The solution:**

CongoFarmers is a web platform that gives every coffee cooperative in the Congo
Agri Platform network a professional digital identity.

Each cooperative has a shareable profile page — accessible from a URL or a
QR code — showing:
- **Who they are:** region, altitude, member count, certifications
- **How their coffee tastes:** a sensory radar chart built from professional
  cupping data
- **Their track record:** Best of Congo competition scores, quantities sold,
  and the international roasters who bought from them
- **How to reach them:** a structured sample request form that routes directly
  to the Congo Agri Platform team

Buyers can scan a QR code at a trade show, open the profile on their phone,
and submit a sample request — in under two minutes, in French or English.

**The market:**

The global specialty coffee market is valued at over $50 billion and growing
at 11% annually. Origin transparency and traceability are no longer a premium
feature — they are the baseline expectation of every serious specialty buyer.

Congo is one of the last underdeveloped origin stories in specialty coffee.
The cooperatives exist. The quality exists. The competition infrastructure
— Best of Congo — exists. What has been missing is the digital layer that
connects all of it to the world.

**What we've built:**

CongoFarmers is live and serving the Congo Agri Platform's network of
50+ cooperatives across the Kivu region. The platform is fully bilingual
(French and English), mobile-optimised, and deployed on cloud infrastructure.

**The ask:**

We are looking for partners — buyers, roasters, NGOs, and impact investors —
who believe that exceptional coffee should find its market regardless of geography.

CongoFarmers is the infrastructure that makes that possible.

## 3. FULL PITCH — 5 minutes
*(Board presentation, investor deck narrative, keynote introduction)*

### The Origin Problem

The specialty coffee world has spent two decades discovering Ethiopia, Colombia,
Guatemala, and Rwanda. These origins have benefited from investment in traceability,
competition infrastructure, and digital presence. Buyers know them. Roasters
source from them. Consumers recognise them.

The Democratic Republic of Congo is different.

The DRC sits at the heart of the Great Rift Valley, with volcanic soils, consistent
rainfall, and altitudes that stretch from 1,400 to over 2,000 metres. The Kivu
region — bordering Rwanda and Burundi, two of specialty coffee's most celebrated
origins — produces coffee of comparable complexity and quality. Bourbon varieties.
Heirloom genetics. Fully washed processing. Cupping scores regularly above 84 points.

The coffee is extraordinary. The visibility has been nearly zero.

### Why

The barrier is not agricultural. It is not financial. It is structural.

Cooperatives in the DRC do not have the digital infrastructure that buyers in
London, New York, Busan, and São Paulo rely on to make sourcing decisions.
There is no origin directory. There is no competition leaderboard that a buyer
can bookmark. There is no profile page that a cooperative can share at a trade
show. There is no structured way for a buyer to say "I want a sample."

Congo Agri Platform — the organisation that has spent years building the
Best of Congo competition, training cooperative managers, and brokering
relationships between DRC producers and international specialty buyers —
has been doing this work with PDF files, email attachments, and manual
introductions.

That is what CongoFarmers replaces.

### The Platform

CongoFarmers is a digital directory and relationship platform built specifically
for the Congo Agri Platform's cooperative network.

**For buyers:**

Every cooperative has a profile page that answers the five questions a sourcing
buyer asks before picking up the phone:

1. Where is this coffee grown, and at what altitude?
2. What certifications does this cooperative hold?
3. What does this coffee taste like? (Sensory radar chart from professional cuppings)
4. Have they competed? What were their scores? Who bought from them before?
5. How do I request a sample?

Each profile is available at a permanent URL and as a QR code — printable for
trade show materials, tasting cards, and sample bag labels. The profile is fully
bilingual. It loads in under two seconds on a mobile phone with no login required.

**For the Best of Congo competition:**

The platform integrates the Best of Congo competition directly into cooperative
profiles. Edition results — scores from four professional jury panels across
London, New York, Goma, and Brazil — appear as a year-by-year performance chart
on every participating cooperative's profile. Buyers can see not just a single
cupping score, but a trajectory: this cooperative scored 84 in 2022, 86 in 2023,
88 in 2024. They are improving. They are investable.

**For Congo Agri Platform:**

Every sample request submitted through the platform flows into a structured
lead database. The platform team can see who requested what, from which country,
and at what quantity — turning cold inbound interest into a prioritised pipeline.

### The Business Case

The economics of specialty coffee origin development are compelling.

A cooperative that sells green coffee at commodity price receives $1.50–2.00 per
pound. A cooperative with verified competition scores, a visible profile, and an
established relationship with a specialty roaster can command $4–8 per pound —
or more for auction lots. The difference is not in the cup. The difference is in
the infrastructure of trust and visibility.

CongoFarmers is that infrastructure.

For Congo Agri Platform, the platform transforms a relationship-driven, manual
brokerage process into a scalable digital operation. For cooperatives, it means
being visible to buyers they will never meet in person. For buyers, it means
access to an origin that has been producing exceptional coffee in obscurity for
decades.

### Traction

- **50+ cooperative profiles** live on the platform
- **Bilingual** French and English with automatic browser language detection
- **Best of Congo integration** with multi-year competition data and jury scores
- **QR code generation** for every profile — trade show ready
- **Sample request pipeline** with structured buyer intake
- **Cloud deployment** on Railway with 99.9% uptime SLA via nginx
- **Zero login required** for buyers — friction-free first contact

### What We Are Building Next

- Lead management dashboard for Congo Agri Platform staff
- Social meta tags and link previews (WhatsApp, LinkedIn, email)
- Buyer analytics: profile view counts, referrer tracking, geographic demand mapping
- Lot availability: real-time inventory per cooperative per harvest season
- Multilingual expansion: Swahili for local cooperative engagement

### The Ask

CongoFarmers is seeking partners across three dimensions:

**Commercial partners:** specialty roasters and importers who want early access
to the Congo Agri Platform network and are ready to build direct relationships
with DRC cooperatives.

**Institutional partners:** development finance institutions, NGOs, and impact
investors who understand that origin infrastructure is as important as origin
quality in building a sustainable specialty coffee supply chain.

**Technical partners:** organisations working in coffee traceability, blockchain
provenance, or supply chain visibility who want to integrate with an active,
growing DRC origin data layer.

The coffee exists. The cooperatives exist. The competition infrastructure exists.
CongoFarmers is the layer that makes it all discoverable.

## 3. PITCH COMPLET — 5 minutes
*(Présentation au conseil, narration de dossier investisseur, introduction en keynote)*

### Le problème de l'origine

Le monde du café de spécialité a passé deux décennies à découvrir l'Éthiopie,
la Colombie, le Guatemala et le Rwanda. Ces origines ont bénéficié
d'investissements dans la traçabilité, les infrastructures de compétition et
la présence numérique. Les acheteurs les connaissent. Les torréfacteurs s'y
approvisionnent. Les consommateurs les reconnaissent.

La République Démocratique du Congo, c'est différent.

La RDC est au cœur du Grand Rift, avec des sols volcaniques, des précipitations
constantes et des altitudes allant de 1 400 à plus de 2 000 mètres. La région
du Kivu — frontalière du Rwanda et du Burundi, deux des origines les plus
célébrées du café de spécialité — produit un café d'une complexité et d'une
qualité comparables. Variétés Bourbon. Génétique héritée. Transformation fully
washed. Des scores de dégustation régulièrement supérieurs à 84 points.

Le café est extraordinaire. La visibilité a été quasi nulle.

### Pourquoi ?

Le frein n'est pas agricole. Il n'est pas financier. Il est structurel.

Les coopératives de RDC ne disposent pas de l'infrastructure numérique sur
laquelle les acheteurs de Londres, New York, Busan et São Paulo s'appuient
pour prendre leurs décisions d'approvisionnement. Il n'existe pas de répertoire
d'origine. Pas de classement de compétition qu'un acheteur puisse mettre en
favori. Pas de page de profil qu'une coopérative puisse partager lors d'un
salon professionnel. Pas de moyen structuré pour qu'un acheteur dise
"je veux un échantillon."

Congo Agri Platform — l'organisation qui, depuis des années, bâtit le concours
Best of Congo, forme les gérants de coopératives et joue les intermédiaires
entre les producteurs congolais et les acheteurs internationaux — effectue
tout ce travail avec des fichiers PDF, des pièces jointes par e-mail et des
présentations manuelles.

C'est ce que CongoFarmers remplace.

### La plateforme

CongoFarmers est une plateforme de répertoire et de mise en relation conçue
spécifiquement pour le réseau de coopératives de Congo Agri Platform.

**Pour les acheteurs :**

Chaque coopérative dispose d'une page de profil qui répond aux cinq questions
qu'un acheteur se pose avant de décrocher son téléphone :

1. Où ce café est-il cultivé, et à quelle altitude ?
2. Quelles certifications cette coopérative détient-elle ?
3. Quel goût a ce café ? (Radar sensoriel issu de dégustations professionnelles)
4. Ont-ils participé à des compétitions ? Quels scores ? Qui les a achetés ?
5. Comment demander un échantillon ?

Chaque profil est disponible via une URL permanente et sous forme de QR code —
imprimable pour les matériaux de salon, les fiches de dégustation et les
étiquettes de sacs d'échantillons. Le profil est entièrement bilingue.
Il se charge en moins de deux secondes sur un téléphone mobile, sans
inscription requise.

**Pour le concours Best of Congo :**

La plateforme intègre directement les résultats du concours Best of Congo dans
les profils des coopératives. Les éditions — scores de quatre jurys professionnels
répartis entre Londres, New York, Goma et le Brésil — apparaissent sous forme
de courbe de performance annuelle sur le profil de chaque coopérative participante.
Les acheteurs voient non pas un simple score de dégustation, mais une trajectoire :
cette coopérative a obtenu 84 en 2022, 86 en 2023, 88 en 2024. Elle progresse.
Elle est investissable.

**Pour Congo Agri Platform :**

Chaque demande d'échantillon soumise via la plateforme alimente une base de
données de prospects structurée. L'équipe de la plateforme peut voir qui a
demandé quoi, de quel pays, et pour quelle quantité — transformant un intérêt
entrant informel en un pipeline priorisé.

### Le modèle économique

L'économie du développement d'origines de café de spécialité est convaincante.

Une coopérative qui vend du café vert au prix marché reçoit 1,50 à 2,00 dollars
la livre. Une coopérative avec des scores de compétition vérifiés, un profil
visible et une relation établie avec un torréfacteur de spécialité peut
prétendre à 4 à 8 dollars la livre — voire davantage pour les lots aux enchères.
La différence ne se trouve pas dans la tasse. Elle se trouve dans l'infrastructure
de confiance et de visibilité.

CongoFarmers est cette infrastructure.

Pour Congo Agri Platform, la plateforme transforme un processus de courtage
manuel, fondé sur les relations, en une opération numérique évolutive. Pour
les coopératives, elle signifie être visible auprès d'acheteurs qu'elles ne
rencontreront jamais en personne. Pour les acheteurs, elle offre un accès à
une origine qui produit un café exceptionnel dans l'ombre depuis des décennies.
