## Post 1 — Launch Announcement

---

For years, introducing a DRC cooperative to a specialty coffee buyer meant attaching a PDF. A static document. No updates. No link. Nothing to share at a cupping table.

Today, every cooperative in our network has its own page on CongoFarmers.

One link. Opens on any phone. No login. No app. Just the story, the data, and a direct way to start a conversation.

CA OLAME on Idjwi Island. 1,245 farmers. 1,450–1,750m. Organic in conversion. Score: 85.

That page now lives at a URL a buyer can bookmark, forward to their sourcing team, or pull up in a meeting.

The PDF catalog served us well. But a live page that reflects current data and connects directly to the producer team is what buyers in 2026 actually need.

## Post 2 — Trade Show / QR Code Feature
**Image:** `03-qr-code-modal.png`

---

**You're at Busan. A buyer asks: "Do you have something I can look at later?"**

Now the answer is yes — and it takes 5 seconds.

Every cooperative profile on CongoFarmers generates a QR code. Print it on a card. Put it on a tasting table. Stick it on a sample bag.

The buyer scans. They land on the full profile: altitude, certifications, sensory profile, Best of Congo competition history, and a direct contact button.

No app to download. No login. No friction.

We built this specifically for the moment after a conversation at an origin event — when the buyer walks away and you want them to remember more than a name on a business card.

The QR code is downloadable as a print-ready SVG from any cooperative's profile page.

→ congofarmers.app

**Image:** `01-public-profile-mobile.png`

---

**What a specialty coffee buyer sees when you share a CongoFarmers profile link:**

Name. Region. Altitude range. Cupping score. Certifications. Member count.

## Post 4 — Request Sample / Lead Generation
**Image:** `04-request-sample-form.png`

---

**"I'm interested" used to mean a cold email with no context.**

Now it means a structured request that lands directly in our system:

→ Buyer's name and company
→ Country
→ Interest level: sample request, contract sourcing, or more information
→ Approximate quantity
→ A message with any specific requirements

Every cooperative profile on CongoFarmers has a "Request Sample" button. The buyer fills it out in 60 seconds. We get a qualified lead with enough context to respond meaningfully.

No email client. No copy-pasting the coop's name into a subject line. No guessing what the buyer actually wants.

For Congo Agri Platform, this changes how we triage inbound buyer interest — from unstructured cold emails to a pipeline we can actually manage.

For buyers, it's the fastest way to start a real sourcing conversation with a DRC producer.

🌱 #CongoCoffee #SpecialtyCoffee #CoffeeProducer #DirectTrade #DRCongo #CoffeeImporter

## Post 6 — Platform Vision / Director-Level Framing
*(No screenshot required — text-only thought leadership)*

---

**Three months ago I was at the Busan International Coffee Forum.**

The panel question: why does DRC coffee have quality but limited market presence?

The answer isn't quality. The scores are there. The altitude is there. The varieties are there.

The answer is visibility. A buyer can't source what they can't find, evaluate, or share with their team.

We've been working on that.

CongoFarmers now gives every cooperative in our network a shareable digital profile — a page a buyer can open, forward, and come back to. A QR code for events. A request form that starts a real sourcing conversation.

The Best of Congo competition produces the credibility. The platform creates the visibility.

Those two things together are what move DRC coffee from "heard of it" to "regularly on our menu."

The work continues.

→ congofarmers.app
